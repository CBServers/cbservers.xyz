The launcher

Point it at a Steam install or let it download the files. It keeps the client and the game current, verifies what you have and launches straight into the mode you pick.

  • Always up to dateClients and game files update in the background, with fast hash-based verification when something breaks.
  • Friends and invitesSee who is playing what, invite people straight into your match, and use Discord or a launcher account, your choice.
  • Mods and custom mapsBrowse the Steam Workshop for Black Ops 3 and hosted catalogs for other games, then install with one click.
